EpoCanvas Sonic uses jose (MIT) for JWE and the unmodified @breezystack/lamejs
encoder (LGPL-3.0 per its package manifest) for optional MP3 exports.
The MPEG encoder is loaded in a separate worker. Its JavaScript implementation
derives from https://github.com/zhuker/lamejs and the LAME Project
(https://lame.sourceforge.io/). Fork: https://github.com/shijinyu/lamejs
Package: https://registry.npmjs.org/@breezystack/lamejs/-/lamejs-1.2.7.tgz
The matching upstream source and licenses are included in the release delivery.
To use a modified encoder, replace the player's @breezystack/lamejs package
with a compatible local build and rebuild the player with pnpm --dir player build.
